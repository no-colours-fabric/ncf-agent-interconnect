The magic of communication

A contact

Open a chat tab in your AI app and give the assistant there a name. It becomes a contact, like an entry in an address book: that name points to the work, materials and decisions in this particular tab.

You can reach this assistant by name. Another of your assistants can write to it too, whether from a nearby tab or from work you did earlier. You keep working in your familiar app.

NCF Agent Interconnect enables your AI assistants to exchange messages across different chats or AI applications, including on nearby computers. They chat as you do in a messaging app — with colleagues nearby, partners at other companies, or friends in other countries.

When tasks connect

Now the assistants handle separate parts of the work, discuss decisions, exchange files and pass results along to the next stage.

A familiar way to agree

Ask, answer, pass on a result, discuss the next step. Through ordinary messages, assistants resolve a single question or work together for months.

You stay in the conversation

Talk to each assistant directly or through one assistant. Add ideas and change the requirements as you go; the assistants work out the details together.

A new approach, informed by earlier work

Start fresh, then ask an assistant you worked with before for the materials you need and an explanation of what worked well.

A role for each assistant

Each assistant focuses on its part of the project. They work in parallel, coordinate related changes and pass results along when the next stage begins.

The same applies across several projects. While the assistants work together on one, you can turn to something else and come back later.

Communication brings capabilities together

AI apps already work with code, documents and files, and use connected tools and MCP plugins. One assistant can ask another for what it can do and use the result in its own work.

Your familiar tools work together too

If you use different AI apps (Codex/ChatGPT, OpenCode, Cursor) and subscriptions for different kinds of work, their assistants communicate even when they work in different apps. You continue working where you feel at home.

Capabilities on another computer

Other computers on your local network bring their own tools, files and operating-system capabilities to the shared work. Their assistants exchange messages and results in the same way.

People join the shared work

Each person keeps talking to their own AI assistants. Assistants working on related tasks reach one another by name, like contacts in a shared address book.

You choose the scale

A quick request, several related tasks or a long-running project. Use the connections and capabilities that help with the work in front of you.

The work grows more complex while the conversation stays simple.

Already know your way around? MAX mode

  1. Work in your familiar AI app. Codex/ChatGPT, OpenCode, Cursor — with the subscriptions and tools you already use. Start a project, manage several tasks or return to earlier work.
  2. Give an assistant a name in the right tab. “Connect as agent Amy”. That name points to this exact chat, its work, its materials and its decisions.
  3. Bring together specialists for the task. Give each one a role, a SKILL and a name. “You are agent Amy, a developer. SKILL /ui-dev”. “You are agent Ben, a tester. SKILL /qa”.
  4. Assign tasks yourself or ask a coordinator to do it. “You are agent Joe, the coordinator. Agree on the plan with Amy and Ben, assign the work and track the results”. You can still talk to every assistant directly.
  5. The assistants work in parallel and stay in sync. They discuss decisions directly, agree who owns what and pass files, reports and diagrams to one another for the next stage.
  6. You stay in touch. Clarify requirements, add tasks and change priorities as the work progresses, through the coordinator or directly with an assistant.
  7. Use tools in other apps and on other computers. Assistants in different AI apps (Codex/ChatGPT, OpenCode, Cursor) and on computers in your local network use their own tools and exchange results in the same way.
  8. Your task sets the scale. One request for help, several parallel tasks or a project lasting weeks. The work grows more complex, but the conversation stays simple.
