# 10. Development and verification across operating systems

LLM-optimized documentation: [llms.txt](https://github.com/no-colours-fabric/ncf-agent-interconnect/blob/main/llms.txt).

Follow a diagnosis from the computer where a problem occurs to the implementer, then back for verification.

- **Topic:** Platform-specific work.
- **People in the example:** 2 human owners for implementation and Windows acceptance.
- **Agent chats in the example:** 2 AI agents.
- **Example environment:** Cedar: Mac development; Atlas: Windows verification.
